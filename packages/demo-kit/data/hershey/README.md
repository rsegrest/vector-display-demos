# Hershey fonts

Stroke fonts in James Hurt's `.jhf` format, taken from https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts.
`tools/build-hershey.mjs` turns them into `packages/demo-kit/src/hersheyData.ts`.

The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the
U. S. National Bureau of Standards. They may be freely used and redistributed with this
acknowledgement, and may not be converted to or distributed in the U.S. NTIS format.
