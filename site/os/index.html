After a short power-on sequence, which any key skips, a desktop appears. Click a program's name in the list on the left to open it in a window. Drag a window by its title bar to move it, drag its bottom right corner to resize it, and click the box at the left of its title bar to close it; the box at the right of a demo's title bar opens that demo full screen. Typing goes to the window in front. In the terminal, type HELP for a list of commands, such as RUN LENIA or SET PERSISTENCE 200. Escape returns to the menu. Every demo here also has a page of its own, listed on the menu.
