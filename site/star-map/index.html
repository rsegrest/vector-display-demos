Time and Location, at the top right, set when and where the sky is seen from: a date and an hour slider, and a city, your browser's location or a typed latitude and longitude. The Sun, Moon with its phase, and planets are shown; P hides them. Drag or use the arrow keys to look around; the mouse wheel or minus and plus zoom. Comma and period change how fast time runs; zero returns to now. L constellation lines, K names, M Milky Way, G grid, B ground, H toggle overlay, F fullscreen, Escape back to the menu.
