Satellite orbits come from CelesTrak and earthquakes from the United States Geological Survey; if they cannot be reached, a bundled snapshot is shown. Keys: drag to rotate, wheel to zoom, point at a satellite or earthquake for details, I follow the space station, minus and plus change how fast time runs, 0 return to now, W replay the past week, S satellites, Q earthquakes, G grid, D day and night line, A spin, space pause, H toggle overlay, F fullscreen, Escape back to the menu.
