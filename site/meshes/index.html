Keys: drag to rotate, wheel to zoom, N and B next and previous mesh, M switch between feature edges and all edges, L show or remove hidden lines, left and right brackets change the crease angle, A auto cycle, C auto camera, T trails, space pause, H toggle overlay, F fullscreen, Escape back to the menu. Models: Utah teapot by Martin Newell; Stanford bunny from the Stanford 3D Scanning Repository; Spot by Keenan Crane.
